# 🌃 2025-03-10_the_last_night [Strength: High]
This file serves as the central entity for the event known as "The Last Night," detailing the final confrontation at Dr. Jekyll's laboratory, culminating in the discovery of Edward Hyde's body and Jekyll's final communications.

/START
### 📜 Event Summary [ALWAYS_LOAD] [Strength: High]
*   **[Event Type]** Investigation & Confrontation | **[Location]** Dr. Jekyll's House & Laboratory | **[Outcome]** Discovery of Hyde's suicide and Jekyll's disappearance/confession.
    ↳ **Initiator:** Poole, Dr. Jekyll's butler, arrives at Mr. Utterson's home in a state of terror, convinced of "foul play" concerning his master.
    ↳ **Climax:** Utterson and Poole forcibly break down the door to Jekyll's cabinet after hearing Hyde's voice from within.
    ↳ **Resolution:** Inside, they find the body of Edward Hyde, dead by suicide. A search reveals no trace of Jekyll, but uncovers a new will, a final note from Jekyll, and a sealed confession, which Utterson takes to read.
/END 📜 Event Summary

### 👥 Participants & Roles [Strength: High]
*   **Mr. Gabriel John Utterson:** The lead investigator and decision-maker. He is summoned by Poole, assesses the evidence, and makes the call to break down the cabinet door. He takes custody of Jekyll's final documents.
*   **Poole:** The catalyst for the event. As Jekyll's butler of twenty years, his testimony about the changed voice, the masked figure's dwarfish stature, and the desperate search for a specific chemical convinces Utterson to act. He assists in the break-in with an axe.
*   **Edward Hyde:** The entity found inside the cabinet. His voice is heard begging for mercy before the break-in. His body is discovered "sorely contorted and still twitching," dressed in Jekyll's oversized clothes. Cause of death is suicide via a "crushed phial" and the "strong smell of kernels" (cyanide).
*   **Dr. Henry Jekyll:** The absent subject of the investigation. Poole believes he was "made away with" eight days prior. His presence is only felt through his handwriting on a desperate note for drugs, a new will, and a final letter to Utterson.
*   **Bradshaw:** Jekyll's footman. He is tasked by Utterson to stand guard with a stick at the laboratory's street door to prevent any escape.
*   **Household Servants:** The entire staff (cook, housemaid, knife-boy, etc.) are found "huddled together like a flock of sheep" in the hall, paralyzed by fear.
/END 👥 Participants & Roles

### ⏳ Chronology of the Confrontation [Strength: High]
*   **Summons:** Poole visits Utterson, reporting that a strange entity has been locked in Jekyll's cabinet for a week, crying out for a specific medicine and that the voice is not his master's.
*   **Testimony:** At Jekyll's house, Poole recounts hearing the occupant "weeping like a woman or a lost soul" and having glimpsed a small, masked figure who "cried out like a rat" and fled. He is certain it was Mr. Hyde.
*   **The Siege:** Utterson, convinced Jekyll has been murdered and the killer is still inside, resolves to force entry. He and Poole arm themselves with a poker and an axe, respectively, and post Bradshaw to guard the rear.
*   **The Final Demand:** Utterson calls out to Jekyll, demanding entry. A voice, which Utterson recognizes as Hyde's, replies, "Utterson, for God's sake, have mercy!"
*   **The Break-in:** Utterson orders Poole to "Down with the door." Poole swings the axe five times, shattering the lock and door, accompanied by a "dismal screech, as of mere animal terror" from within.
/END ⏳ Chronology of the Confrontation

### 🔍 Discoveries within the Cabinet [Strength: High]
*   **The Body of Hyde:** Found in the center of the room, dead by suicide. He is wearing clothes belonging to the much larger Dr. Jekyll.
*   **Absence of Jekyll:** A thorough search of the entire laboratory building, including the cellar, reveals no trace of Henry Jekyll, dead or alive. The by-street door is locked from the inside, with a rusted and broken key on the floor.
*   **The Documents:** Found on the business table in a large envelope addressed to Utterson:
    ↳ **New Will:** A revised testament, identical to the old one but with "Gabriel John Utterson" named as the sole beneficiary instead of Edward Hyde.
    ↳ **Jekyll's Final Note:** A brief, dated letter confirming Jekyll's impending "disappearance" and instructing Utterson to first read Dr. Lanyon's narrative, and then his own confession.
    ↳ **The Full Confession:** A "considerable packet sealed in several places," which Utterson takes with him.
*   **Anomalous Items:**
    ↳ A pious book, esteemed by Jekyll, is found open and annotated with "startling blasphemies" in his handwriting.
    ↳ A cheval-glass (full-length mirror) which Poole asserts "has seen some strange things."
    ↳ Traces of chemical work, with measured heaps of a white salt, indicating a recently failed experiment.
/END 🔍 Discoveries within the Cabinet

### 🌬️ Atmosphere & Setting [Strength: Medium]
*   **External Environment:** A "wild, cold, seasonable night of March" with a pale moon and flying clouds. The wind makes talking difficult and the streets are unusually deserted, filling Utterson with a "crushing anticipation of calamity."
*   **Internal Tension:** The brightly lit hall of Jekyll's house contrasts with the terrified servants huddled by the fire. The silence of the house is broken only by the "lightly and oddly" pacing footfall from the cabinet above.
*   **The Cabinet's Paradox:** Post-break-in, the room is found in a state of profound quiet: a fire glows, a kettle sings, and tea things are laid out, creating a jarringly mundane scene for the violent discovery.
