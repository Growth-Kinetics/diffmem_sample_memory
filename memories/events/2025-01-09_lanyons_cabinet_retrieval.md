*   **[Date Received]** January 9th, 18— | **[Delivery Method]** Evening post, registered envelope.
*   **[Sender]** Dr. Henry Jekyll | **[Recipient]** Dr. Hastie Lanyon.

    ↳ **Quote:** "if you fail me to-night, I am lost."
*   **[Instructions Part 1]** Postpone all engagements, travel to Jekyll's house, meet Poole and a locksmith, force the cabinet door, and retrieve the fourth drawer from press "E".

### 🚪 **Action: The Cabinet Breach** [Strength: Low]
*   **[Lanyon's Motivation]** Believes Jekyll is insane but feels an appeal "so worded could not be set aside without a grave responsibility."
*   **[Location]** Dr. Jekyll's House.
*   **[Collaborators]** Poole (Jekyll's butler), a locksmith, and a carpenter.
*   **[Process]** The party enters the cabinet via Dr. Denman's old surgical theatre. The locksmith works for two hours to open the strong door.
*   **[Outcome]** Lanyon enters the cabinet alone, retrieves the specified drawer, has it packed in straw and a sheet, and returns with it to his home in Cavendish Square.
/END 🚪 **Action: The Cabinet Breach**

### 🧪 **Analysis: The Retrieved Drawer** [Strength: Low]
*   **[Lanyon's Assessment]** He examines the contents, finding them curious but offering little definite information. He concludes it is a record of experiments with no practical use.
*   **[Contents]**
    ↳ **Powders:** Described as a "simple crystalline salt of a white colour." Noted as being of Jekyll's "private manufacture."
    ↳ **Phial:** About half-full of a "blood-red liquor," highly pungent, and believed to contain "phosphorus and some volatile ether."
    ↳ **Book:** An ordinary version book containing a series of dates covering many years. Entries ceased abruptly nearly a year ago.
        ↳ **Notable Entries:** The word "double" appears multiple times. An early entry reads "total failure!!!"
/END 🧪 **Analysis: The Retrieved Drawer**

### 👤 **Climax: The Messenger & Transformation** [Strength: Low]
*   **[The Arrival]** At midnight, a small man arrives, acting furtively. Lanyon admits him while armed with a revolver.
*   **[Messenger's Description (Hyde)]** Small, with a "shocking expression," a combination of muscular activity and apparent debility. His clothes are enormously too large. He induces a "disgustful curiosity" and a physical sense of "incipient rigour" in Lanyon.
*   **[The Transaction]** The messenger ("Have you got it?") impatiently receives the drawer. He utters a "loud sob of such immense relief" upon seeing the contents.
*   **[The Metamorphosis]**
    ↳ **Preparation:** The messenger mixes a few minims of the red tincture with one of the powders in a graduated glass. The mixture effervesces and changes color from red, to purple, to a watery green.
    ↳ **The Choice:** He offers Lanyon the choice to let him leave or to witness a "prodigy to stagger the unbelief of Satan." Lanyon chooses to see the end.
    ↳ **The Event:** The messenger drinks the potion. He cries out, reels, seems to swell, and his features "melt and alter." Before Lanyon's eyes, he transforms into Dr. Henry Jekyll.
/END 👤 **Climax: The Messenger & Transformation**

### 💥 **Consequence: The Fatal Shock** [Strength: Low]
*   **[Immediate Impact]** Lanyon is submerged in terror, screaming "O God!".
*   **[Jekyll's Confession]** Jekyll spends the next hour unveiling a "moral turpitude" that sickens Lanyon's soul.
*   **[Key Revelation]** Lanyon learns that the creature who entered his house was "known by the name of Hyde and hunted for in every corner of the land as the murderer of Carew."
*   **[Effect on Lanyon]** His life is "shaken to its roots." He suffers from sleeplessness and a "deadliest terror" and feels his days are numbered, though he will "die incredulous."
