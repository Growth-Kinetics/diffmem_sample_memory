### 📍 Location & Atmosphere [ALWAYS_LOAD] [Strength: Medium]
*   **[Address]** A house located in a "dismal quarter of Soho."
*   **[Neighborhood]** Described by Mr. Utterson as a "nightmare" district. The "dingy street" features a gin palace, a low French eating house, and a shop for penny numbers. It is populated by "ragged children" and "women of many different nationalities."
*   **[Atmosphere]** Characterized by "muddy ways," "slatternly passengers," and a pervasive, "chocolate-coloured" fog that creates a gloomy, twilight-like environment. Utterson perceives it as "blackguardly surroundings."

    ↳ **Amenities:** A closet is filled with wine, the plate is silver, and the napery is elegant.
    ↳ **Decor:** The carpets are of "many plies and agreeable in colour." A "good picture" hangs on the walls, which Utterson presumes is a gift from the connoisseur, Dr. Henry Jekyll.

### 🔍 Crime Scene Evidence [Strength: High]

*   **[Recovered Evidence]**
    ↳ **Financial:** From the embers, Inspector Newcomen disinters the "butt end of a green cheque book." A subsequent bank visit reveals several thousand pounds credited to Hyde.

/END 🔍 Crime Scene Evidence
