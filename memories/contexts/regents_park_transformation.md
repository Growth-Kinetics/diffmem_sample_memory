# 🌀 regents_park_transformation Integration System [Strength: Low]
This file serves as the central entity for the spontaneous transformation of Dr. Jekyll into Mr. Hyde in Regent's Park. It documents the turning point where the change became involuntary, marking the final loss of control.

/START
### 🌟 **Event Summary & Significance** [ALWAYS_LOAD] [Strength: High]
*   **[Event]** The Regent's Park Transformation.
*   **[Type]** Involuntary physical and psychological transformation.
*   **[Summary]** While sitting on a park bench in a moment of self-satisfied pride, Dr. Jekyll spontaneously transformed into Mr. Hyde without the use of the potion. This event occurred in public, during the day, leaving him stranded in ill-fitting clothes, a known murderer, and cut off from the drugs in his cabinet.
*   **[Significance]** This is the critical turning point where the balance of power permanently shifts to Hyde. It marks the end of Jekyll's ability to control the changes, making his original identity a fugitive state only accessible via the potion. This forces Hyde to take action, leading him to contact Dr. Lanyon and set in motion the final revelations of the story.
/END 🌟 **Event Summary & Significance**

### ⛓️ **Causal Chain & Sequence** [Strength: Medium]
*   **[Preceding Context]** After two months of strict self-control following the Carew murder, Jekyll had begun to relax his vigilance. He was sitting on a bench in Regent's Park, indulging in a "vainglorious thought" comparing his own "active good-will" to the "lazy cruelty" of others.
*   **[Trigger]** The transformation was initiated at the "very moment of that vainglorious thought," suggesting a psychological or moral catalyst rather than a chemical one.
*   **[Transformation Sequence]**
    ↳ **Phase 1 (Physical Sensation):** A sudden "qualm," "horrid nausea," and "the most deadly shuddering."
    ↳ **Phase 2 (Psychological Shift):** Faintness gives way to a "change in the temper of my thoughts," characterized by "a greater boldness, a contempt of danger, a solution of the bonds of obligation."
    ↳ **Phase 3 (Physical Manifestation):** Jekyll looks down to see his clothes hanging loosely on "shrunken limbs" and his hand transformed into the "corded and hairy" hand of Edward Hyde.
*   **[Immediate Realization]** A moment of pure horror as he comprehends his new state: from a wealthy, beloved doctor to "the common quarry of mankind, hunted, houseless, a known murderer, thrall to the gallows."
/END ⛓️ **Causal Chain & Sequence**

### 🏃 **Consequential Actions (Hyde's Response)** [Strength: Medium]
*   **[Problem Assessment]** Hyde's "sharpened" faculties immediately grasp the situation: he is stranded, the laboratory door is locked, and his own servants would turn him in to be hanged. Accessing the drugs in the cabinet is the paramount goal.
*   **[Solution Formulation]** Realizing he must use an intermediary, he thinks of Dr. Lanyon. His key advantage is that he still possesses Jekyll's handwriting.
*   **[Plan Execution]**
    ↳ **1. Transport:** Summons a hansom cab, suppressing a "gust of devilish fury" at the driver's mirth.
    ↳ **2. Secure Location:** Goes to a hotel on Portland Street, intimidating the staff with his demeanor to secure a private room and writing materials.
    ↳ **3. Communication:** Composes and sends two "important letters" via registered post: one to Lanyon with instructions to retrieve the drugs, and one to Poole.
    ↳ **4. Waiting:** Sits all day in the hotel room "gnawing his nails," before wandering the city streets at night in a closed cab and on foot, eventually making his way to Lanyon's house.
/END 🏃 **Consequential Actions (Hyde's Response)**
