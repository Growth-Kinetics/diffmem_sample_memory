### 🔒 **Access & Sealing Conditions** [Strength: High]
*   **Outer Packet Superscription:** "PRIVATE: for the hands of G. J. Utterson ALONE, and in case of his predecease to be destroyed unread."
    ↳ **Implication:** Establishes a bond of absolute personal trust and secrecy between Lanyon and Utterson. The information is deemed too sensitive for anyone else.
*   **Inner Enclosure Inscription:** "not to be opened till the death or disappearance of Dr. Henry Jekyll."
    ↳ **Implication:** The contents directly concern Dr. Jekyll and are only to be revealed once he is no longer present. The term "disappearance" creates a direct and disturbing link to the terms of Jekyll's will.
*   **Custodian's Obligation:** Despite "a great curiosity," Mr. Utterson's sense of "professional honour and faith to his dead friend" are "stringent obligations" that prevent him from violating the seals.

### 🧠 **Context & Significance** [Strength: Medium]
*   **Origin Context:** Lanyon, on his deathbed following a great "shock," refused to see or speak of Jekyll, whom he regarded "as dead." He promised Utterson that "after I am dead, you may perhaps come to learn the right and wrong of this." This narrative is the promised explanation.
*   **Thematic Link to Jekyll's Will:** The condition of "disappearance" echoes the clause in Jekyll's will that would transfer his fortune to Hyde. Utterson is horrified to see this idea, which he associated with the "sinister suggestion of the man Hyde," now written "by the hand of Lanyon." This deepens the mystery and suggests Lanyon learned something that confirmed the will's strange provision.
*   **Psychological Impact on Utterson:** The existence of the unopened packet transforms Utterson's relationship with Jekyll. His thoughts of his friend become "disquieted and fearful." He is now "relieved to be denied admittance" to Jekyll's home, preferring the safety of the doorstep to the "house of voluntary bondage" and its "inscrutable recluse." The document represents a source of constant, underlying dread.
/END 🧠 **Context & Significance**
