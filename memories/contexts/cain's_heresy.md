# 🧿 Cain's Heresy Integration System [Strength: Low]
This file serves as the central entity for Cain's Heresy, a personal philosophy of radical tolerance and non-interference, as articulated by Mr. Utterson.

/START
### 📜 Core Definition & Tenets [ALWAYS_LOAD] [Strength: High]
*   **[Verbatim Quote]** "I let my brother go to the devil in his own way."
*   **[Core Principle]** A philosophy of radical non-interference and tolerance for the choices of others, even when those choices lead to ruin or "misdeeds."
*   **[Key Tenet]** An inclination to "help rather than to reprove," offering support without judgment.
/END 📜 Core Definition & Tenets

/START
### 🎭 Manifestation & Practice [Strength: Medium]
*   **[Primary Practitioner]** Mr. Gabriel John Utterson.
*   **[Observed Behavior]** Maintaining an unwavering and unchanged demeanor towards individuals in decline ("downgoing men"). He provides a stable, non-judgmental presence.
*   **[Internal Attitude]** Involves a quiet curiosity, "almost with envy," at the energy and "high pressure of spirits" involved in others' transgressions, rather than moral condemnation.
*   **[Corollary Principle - Enfield]** Mr. Richard Enfield articulates a similar principle of deliberate non-inquiry: "I make it a rule of mine: the more it looks like Queer Street, the less I ask." This is seen as a "very good rule" by Utterson, reinforcing the theme of avoiding judgment.
/END 🎭 Manifestation & Practice


/START
### ⚠️ Limitations & Conflicts [Strength: Low]
*   **[Conflict with Duty/Concern]** Despite endorsing non-inquiry, Mr. Utterson breaks his own principle when a story "has gone home" (i.e., it personally involves someone he knows or feels responsible for, like the signatory of the cheque).
*   **[Observed Break]** He presses Mr. Enfield for the name of the man who trampled the child (Hyde), demonstrating that personal connection and deep concern can override the general philosophy of non-interference.
/END ⚠️ Limitations & Conflicts

/START
### 🔗 Relational Impact [Strength: Medium]
*   **[Social Function]** This philosophy often results in the practitioner becoming the "last reputable acquaintance and the last good influence" in the lives of troubled individuals.
*   **[Effect]** It creates a safe harbor for those who have strayed, ensuring they have at least one connection to respectable society.
*   **[Known Adherents]:**
    ↳ [Mr. Gabriel John Utterson]
/END 🔗 Relational Impact

/START
### 🏛️ Philosophical Context [Strength: Low]
*   **[Origin]** A "quaint" personal saying of Mr. Utterson, referencing the biblical story of Cain.
*   **[Interpretation]** It is termed a "heresy" because it consciously rejects the societal or moral duty to police another's behavior (i.e., to be one's "brother's keeper"), reframing it as a principle of personal autonomy.
/END 🏛️ Philosophical Context
