### 💥 **The Midnight Transformation** [Strength: High]
*   **The Setup:** Following an urgent request from Henry Jekyll, Dr. Lanyon brings a drawer containing chemicals and a notebook from Jekyll's cabinet to his own home. In preparation for a mysterious midnight visitor, he dismisses his servants and loads an old revolver for self-defense.
*   **The Arrival:** At midnight, a small man (Mr. Hyde) arrives, knocking gently. He is admitted by Lanyon into the consulting room, appearing agitated and fearful of a policeman in the square.
*   **The Experiment:** Inside the consulting room, Hyde impatiently demands the drawer. Using a graduated glass provided by Lanyon, he mixes a measured amount of a "blood-red" tincture with one of the powders. The mixture effervesces, brightens, and changes color from red to purple, then to a "watery green."
*   **The Climax:** After giving Lanyon the choice to witness the event, Hyde drinks the potion. He emits a cry, reels, and undergoes a horrific physical transformation. His body seems to swell, and his features "melt and alter."
*   **The Revelation:** Where Hyde stood, Dr. Henry Jekyll appears, "pale and shaken." The event, which Lanyon later calls a "prodigy to stagger the unbelief of Satan," proves the reality of Jekyll's "transcendental medicine" and becomes the direct cause of Lanyon's fatal decline.
/END 💥 **The Midnight Transformation**
