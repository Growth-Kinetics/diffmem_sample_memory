# 🧿 search_for_the_pure_salt Integration System [Strength: Low]
This file serves as the central entity for the week-long, desperate search for a pure chemical salt conducted from within Dr. Jekyll's cabinet, as reported by Poole.

/START
### 📖 **Event Narrative & Context** [ALWAYS_LOAD] [Strength: High]
*   **Objective:** To acquire a specific chemical salt of a particular "pure" quality, identical to a batch purchased in a previous year (18--).
*   **Subject:** The entity residing in Dr. Jekyll's cabinet (identified by Poole as Hyde, but writing in Jekyll's hand) is "crying night and day for some sort of medicine."
*   **Duration:** The search has been ongoing for approximately one week prior to Poole's visit to Utterson.
*   **Urgency:** The need is described as "bitter bad." The written orders state that "Expense is no consideration" and the matter's importance "can hardly be exaggerated," culminating in a desperate, handwritten plea: "For God's sake, find me some of the old."
/END 📖 **Event Narrative & Context**


/START
### 🎯 **The Critical Flaw (per Jekyll's Confession)** [Strength: High]
*   **Source of Failure:** Jekyll's final confession reveals the frantic search was based on a catastrophic misunderstanding. The original salt, purchased years prior, was effective not because of its purity, but because of an unknown **impurity** it contained.
*   **Jekyll's Realization:** He writes, "I am now persuaded that my first supply was impure, and that it was that unknown impurity which lent efficacy to the draught."
*   **Doomed Search:** All subsequent batches procured by Poole, being chemically pure as requested, lacked this accidental, essential contaminant. The search for a "pure" salt was therefore destined to fail from the outset.
/END 🎯 **The Critical Flaw (per Jekyll's Confession)**


/START
### 🧪 **Original Formula Components (per Lanyon's Account)** [Strength: High]
*   **Source:** A drawer (press E, fourth from top) retrieved from Jekyll's cabinet by Dr. Lanyon on January 9th, at Jekyll's written request.
*   **Component 1 (The Salt):** A large quantity of a particular salt, originally purchased from a firm of wholesale chemists. Dr. Lanyon incorrectly assumed it was of Jekyll's "private manufacture." Jekyll later concludes this original batch contained an unknown **impurity** which was the true active ingredient, a fact that made all subsequent, purer supplies ineffective.
*   **Component 2 (The Tincture):** A phial containing a "blood-red liquor," described as highly pungent and containing phosphorus and some volatile ether.
*   **The Record:** A paper book containing a series of dates covering many years, believed to be a record of Jekyll's experiments with the formula. Entries ceased nearly a year prior to Lanyon's retrieval.
*   **Transformation Process:** Hyde mixes a small amount of the red tincture with one of the powders in a graduated glass. The mixture effervesces, changes color from red to purple to watery green, and is then consumed to trigger the transformation back into Jekyll.
/END 🧪 **Original Formula Components (per Lanyon's Account)**

/START
### ⚙️ **Operational Process** [Strength: Medium]
*   **Command Method:** Orders are written on sheets of paper and thrown down onto the stair from the locked cabinet.
*   **Logistics:** Poole is dispatched "twice and thrice in the same day" to various "wholesale chemists in town" to procure the drug.
*   **Failure Loop:** Upon delivery, each new sample is tested and subsequently rejected. A new paper is thrown down, declaring the sample "impure" and directing Poole to return it and try a different firm.
*   **Physical Evidence:** In the cabinet, "various measured heaps of some white salt being laid on glass saucers" are found, indicating repeated, failed experiments. Poole confirms this is the drug he was sent to fetch.
/END ⚙️ **Operational Process**

/START
### 🔗 **Involved Entities & Locations** [Strength: Medium]
*   **Dr. Jekyll:** The originator of the demand. The notes are "unquestionably the doctor's hand," and he is the one who made the original purchase in 18--.
*   **Poole:** The primary agent, tasked with physically fetching and returning the chemical samples from across London.
*   **The Figure in the Cabinet (Hyde):** The being physically conducting the search from within the cabinet. He is heard pacing and weeping, and is seen by Poole "digging among the crates" before fleeing. Poole believes this figure to be Mr. Hyde.
*   **Messrs. Maw:** One of the wholesale chemists contacted. They received a note from Dr. Jekyll, became "main angry," and threw the note back at Poole, refusing the request.
*   **Dr. Jekyll's Cabinet:** The epicenter of the search, from which all orders emanate and where experiments are conducted.
/END 🔗 **Involved Entities & Locations**

/START
### 🧠 **Behavioral Manifestations** [Strength: Medium]
*   **Pacing:** The figure in the cabinet is heard constantly walking "to and fro along the cabinet floor," day and night. This activity only pauses briefly when a new chemical sample is delivered.
*   **Vocalization:** Poole reports hearing the figure "crying night and day for some sort of medicine."
*   **Weeping:** On one occasion, Poole heard the entity "weeping like a woman or a lost soul," an event that deeply disturbed him.
*   **Isolation:** The figure remains locked in the cabinet, refusing all visitors and having meals left outside the door to be "smuggled in when nobody was looking."

*   **Jekyll's Explanation:** In his final statement, Jekyll anticipates this behavior. He describes that once trapped as Hyde permanently, he will "sit shuddering and weeping" or "pace up and down" in a "fearstruck ecstasy of listening." This confirms the behavior is driven by Hyde's terror of capture and his hatred for the confinement of the cabinet.
/END 🧠 **Behavioral Manifestations**
