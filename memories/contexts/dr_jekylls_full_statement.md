### 📖 Key Revelations & Confession Details [Strength: High]
*   **Philosophical Motivation:** Jekyll confesses his lifelong struggle with a "profound duplicity of life." His scientific studies led him to the truth "that man is not truly one, but truly two." He theorized that separating these two natures would free both from the other—the just could be purely good, and the unjust could pursue pleasure without remorse.
*   **The Transformation:** Jekyll compounded a drug that could "shake and pluck back that fleshly vestment." He describes the first transformation as agonizing but leading to a feeling of being "younger, lighter, happier in body," along with a "heady recklessness." He saw the resulting form, Edward Hyde, in a mirror and felt "a leap of welcome."
*   **The Nature of Hyde:** Hyde is described as smaller, slighter, and younger than Jekyll because Jekyll's evil side had been "much less exercised." He was the embodiment of pure evil, which left an "imprint of deformity and decay" on his body, inspiring revulsion in all who saw him.
*   **Progressive Loss of Control:**
    ↳ **Initial Phase:** Jekyll used Hyde as a "thick cloak" to indulge in "undignified" pleasures with impunity, believing he could always return to his respectable self.
    ↳ **First Involuntary Change:** He awoke one morning as Hyde without having taken the potion, a "Babylonian finger on the wall" that spelled his judgment.
    ↳ **Escalation:** After a two-month period of abstinence, Jekyll's "devil came out roaring," leading to the murder of Sir Danvers Carew.
    ↳ **Final Crisis:** The balance permanently shifts. Jekyll begins transforming into Hyde spontaneously, especially in his sleep. He discovers his original batch of salt had a unique, unknown impurity that made the potion work. Subsequent pure supplies are useless, trapping him.
*   **Final Act:** The statement is written by Jekyll in his last hours, using the last of the original powder. He knows that once it wears off, he will become Hyde permanently and brings "the life of that unhappy Henry Jekyll to an end."
/END 📖 Key Revelations & Confession Details
