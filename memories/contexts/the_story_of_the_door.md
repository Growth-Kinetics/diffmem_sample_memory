# 🧿 The Story of the Door Integration System [Strength: Low]
This file serves as the central entity for the narrative told by Mr. Enfield concerning a sinister door, a violent incident, and a mysterious cheque. It establishes the initial facts and the central mystery of Mr. Hyde's identity and connections.

/START
### 📖 **Narrative Summary** [ALWAYS_LOAD] [Strength: Low]
*   **[Narrator]** Mr. Richard Enfield.
*   **[Audience]** Mr. Gabriel John Utterson.
*   **[Core Event]** In the early hours of a winter morning, a small, detestable man (Mr. Hyde) calmly tramples a young girl and is subsequently cornered by Enfield and a crowd.
*   **[Resolution]** To avoid scandal, Hyde is compelled to pay £100. He retrieves £10 in gold and a cheque for the balance from a mysterious, sordid building using his own key.
*   **[Key Anomaly]** The cheque is genuine and signed by a famously reputable and charitable gentleman, leading to speculation about blackmail.
/END 📖 **Narrative Summary**

/START
### 📍 **Key Locations & Objects** [Strength: Low]
*   **The Door / Building:**
    ↳ **Location:** A bystreet in a busy quarter of London.
    ↳ **Appearance:** A "sinister block of building" two storeys high, with no windows on the ground floor. The door itself is "blistered and distained," without a bell or knocker.
    ↳ **Condition:** Bears marks of "prolonged and sordid negligence."
    ↳ **Nickname:** "Black Mail House" (coined by Enfield).
*   **The Cheque:**
    ↳ **Value:** For the balance of a £100 payment (after £10 in gold).
    ↳ **Signatory:** An unnamed but "very well known" and "celebrated" gentleman, described as the "very pink of the proprieties."
    ↳ **Authenticity:** Confirmed as genuine at the bank, deepening the mystery.
/END 📍 **Key Locations & Objects**

### 👥 **Characters Involved** [Strength: Low]
*   **[Antagonist] Mr. Hyde:**
    ↳ **Key Action:** "Trampled calmly over the child's body," an act described as "hellish to see" and like a "damned Juggernaut."
    ↳ **Demeanor:** "Perfectly cool" with a "black sneering coolness," yet also visibly frightened. Compared to "Satan."
    ↳ **Physicality:** Described as small and "extraordinary looking," but unspecifiable. He gives off a "strong feeling of deformity" that inspires instant loathing, yet has no nameable physical flaw.
*   **[Narrator] Mr. Richard Enfield:**
    ↳ **Role:** Primary witness who collared Hyde and negotiated the payment.
    ↳ **Philosophy:** Avoids asking questions about strange matters ("the more it looks like Queer Street, the less I ask").
*   **[Observer] The Doctor ("Sawbones"):**
    ↳ **Role:** Attended to the trampled child.
    ↳ **Reaction:** Despite a typically unemotional disposition, he turned "sick and white with the desire to kill" Hyde upon seeing him.
*   **[Implicit] The Cheque Signatory:**
    ↳ **Reputation:** A man of impeccable public standing, known for doing good.
    ↳ **Inferred Connection:** His cheque being used by Hyde suggests a hidden, possibly coerced, relationship.
/END 👥 **Characters Involved**

### 🧩 **Central Mystery & Conflicts** [Strength: Low]
*   **The Nature of Hyde:** The primary conflict is understanding Hyde's character. He inspires a universal, physical revulsion in all who see him, which cannot be explained by his appearance.
*   **The Cheque Anomaly:** The core puzzle is how a disreputable figure like Hyde has access to the funds and property (via a key) of a highly respected gentleman.
*   **Blackmail Hypothesis:** Enfield's theory is that the reputable man is being blackmailed for past misdeeds. He himself admits this explanation is insufficient ("far from explaining all").
*   **Utterson's Prior Knowledge:** Mr. Utterson's deep disturbance and admission that he already knows the name of the cheque's signatory indicates a pre-existing connection to the mystery.
/END 🧩 **Central Mystery & Conflicts**
