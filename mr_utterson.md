    ↳ **Poole:** Dr. Jekyll's well-dressed, elderly butler. He confirms Hyde's authority in the household.     ↳ **Sir Danvers Carew:** [Status: Deceased | Client] An aged, white-haired, and well-regarded gentleman—and a client of Utterson's—murdered by Mr. Hyde. By chance, Guest is also able to see a dinner invitation from Jekyll and compares the two scripts. He concludes that the two hands are "in many points identical: only differently sloped," providing the crucial evidence that leads Utterson to believe Jekyll forged the letter.
